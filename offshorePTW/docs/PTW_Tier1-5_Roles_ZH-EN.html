任務說明
RESPONSIBILITIES
TIER1承商 Contractor
承商持有人／申請人 Contractor Holder / Applicant
中文

建立與提交 PTW、指定持有人／副持有人、填寫證書與上傳附件；通過教育訓練後也可擔任現場工單持有人（同一人可兼任申請人與持有人）。HSE 人員不可擔任持有人。

ENGLISH

Creates and submits PTWs, assigns holders / co-holders, completes certificates and uploads attachments; once training is passed, may also act as the on-site permit holder (the same person can be both applicant and holder). HSE personnel cannot act as a holder.

TIER2承商 Contractor
承商職安衛 Contractor HSE
中文

承商內部第一關審查（僅限本公司 PTW）。亦可自行申請 PTW：不得自審，送出後直接進入 Tier 3 審查；HSE 人員不可擔任持有人。

ENGLISH

First-line review within the contractor (own company PTWs only). May also apply for a PTW: no self-review — it goes straight to Tier 3; HSE personnel cannot act as a holder.

TIER3NMDC
NMDC 施工部門 NMDC Construction
中文

施工面審查：施工方法、介面與現場條件。

ENGLISH

Construction review: method, interfaces and site conditions.

TIER4NMDC
NMDC 安衛部門 NMDC HSE
中文

安衛面審查：危害辨識、預防措施與法規符合性。

ENGLISH

HSE review: hazards, precautions and compliance.

TIER5NMDC
PTW 協調員 PTW Coordinator
中文

最終審核並簽發正式 PTW 編號，執行啟用、延長、暫停／恢復與關閉。Tier 5 不等於系統管理員——系統管理權限須由管理員另於「使用者管理」勾選後才取得。

ENGLISH

Final review and issue of the official PTW number; activation, extension, suspension / resumption and close-out. Tier 5 is not the system administrator — administration rights must be granted separately in User Management.

審核順序 Approval sequence：Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 → Tier 4 → Tier 5（簽發 Issue）　｜　Tier 2–5 皆可退回申請人 Any of Tier 2–5 may return to the applicant NMDC Offshore PTW System
